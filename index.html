About

Built for Scale.
Wired for Growth.

I'm a transformational eCommerce and digital business leader with 15+ years building and scaling DTC, Amazon/marketplace, and omnichannel businesses from the ground up — and taking them to $400M+ in digital revenue.

My career spans iconic consumer brands — WPP, IPG, Sears, Newell Brands (Graco, Baby Jogger, Calphalon, Crockpot, Parker Pens), Party City, Dormify, Ripple Junction — where I've owned full Digital P&Ls, built high-performance digital teams, and driven measurable EBITDA impact. I specialize in the hard problems: 0→1 channel launches, turnarounds, omnichannel transformation, and scaling marketplace businesses where economics, execution, and consumer experience have to work together.
