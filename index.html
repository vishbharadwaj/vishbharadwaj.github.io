About

Builder. Strategist. Operator.

My career spans iconic consumer brands — from WPP and IPG agency roots (JWT/Lowe, launching Nike, Levi's, Jockey, and Hanes) to senior operator roles at Sears Holdings, Newell Brands (Graco, Baby Jogger, Calphalon, Crockpot, Parker Pens), Party City, Dormify, and Ripple Junction.

I specialize in building high-performance eCommerce engines from scratch — architecting DTC platforms, scaling Amazon and marketplace businesses, and leading omnichannel transformations that connect digital and physical retail. I've launched brands across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific, and built teams that deliver lasting commercial results.

What sets me apart is the ability to operate at both strategic and executional levels — from boardroom P&L conversations to hands-on platform builds — while developing talent and fostering cultures where digital commerce teams thrive.
